## Competitive read
- Keka and greythr are mature, heavily linked brands. Do not try to match them by volume.
- Realistic near-term benchmark: pockethrms, factohr and qandle (small hosts counts and mid ranks). Moving from rank about 18M to under 1M (qandle level) needs on the order of dozens of quality referring domains, not hundreds.

## Prioritized link-building plan (opportunities, not verified gaps)

### Critical / quick wins (weeks 1-4)
1. Software directories, claim or create profiles with a full description, screenshots and a link to the matching product page (HRMS, CLMS, Visitor Mgmt, Canteen, eMpay): G2, Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice (same Gartner family), SoftwareSuggest, Techjockey, GoodFirms, SourceForge, Crozdesk, TrustRadius, Clutch (if services are offered). Many pass nofollow, but they give brand validation, referral traffic and often one dofollow link. First verify manually whether listings already exist and make NAP and description consistent with the site.
2. Client case studies and testimonials: the site now has /our-clients and /testimonial and client logos. Ask named clients (Mahindra, Nerolac, Kores, Yazaki, Varroc, Vishay, etc., per the repo assets) for a link from their CSR, supplier or press pages, or joint case-study press. Large-brand links are the highest-value, hardest to get; start with the smaller clients.
3. Foundational profiles: LinkedIn company page, Crunchbase, Google Business Profile (Pune), Clutch, Justdial, IndiaMART/TradeIndia (B2B), AngelList/Wellfound, Glassdoor. These are low authority but give consistent entity signals and some links.

### High (months 1-3)
4. Industry associations and chambers: CII, NASSCOM (product company listing or membership directory), MCCIA (Pune chamber), Indian Staffing Federation, NHRDN, SHRM India, AIMA, local MIDC and industry associations (auto-component and manufacturing clusters around Pune, matching the client base). Membership pages and event sponsor pages usually carry links.
5. HR and compliance publications: People Matters, ETHRWorld (Economic Times HR), HRKatha, Business Today, Express Computer, CIO Insight / CXOToday, YourStory, Inc42 (for funding or product news). Pitch data-led angles: Labour Codes compliance (the four labour codes and CLRA contractor compliance are a clear niche where emsphere's CLMS can be an authority), contract labour compliance checklists, canteen and visitor management in factories.
6. Linkable assets on the site: a contract labour compliance guide (CLRA/Labour Codes by state), free calculators (PF/ESI/gratuity/bonus, minimum wages by state), HR templates and checklists, an annual manufacturing workforce compliance report. These attract natural links and are a fit for CLMS positioning, where competitors (keka, greythr) are weaker than in generic HRMS.
7. Expert contributions: HARO-style requests (Qwoted, Featured, Muck Rack, and Indian equivalents), expert quotes by the founder or HR/compliance lead, guest posts on HR/manufacturing sites. Use named authors with bios (also helps E-E-A-T).

### Medium
8. Partner and integration pages: biometric and attendance hardware vendors, ERP and accounting vendors (Tally, SAP B1), payroll and compliance partners, resellers. Ask for a partner listing with a backlink and reciprocate sparingly.
9. Comparison and alternatives listicles: pitch "best HRMS for manufacturing in India" and "contract labour management software" roundup authors. Check who already ranks for these queries and request inclusion.
10. Events, webinars, podcasts: HR-tech conferences (HR Tech India, NHRDN), Pune manufacturing expos. Speaker pages produce links.
11. Press releases on product launches via PTI/ANI-syndicated services. These are mostly nofollow or low-value, so use them for mentions and not as a primary tactic.

### Avoid
- Paid link packages, PBN links, mass directory submission to generic "free listing" sites, exact-match anchor spam. These carry penalty risk and are not needed in this niche.

## Anchor and quality targets
- Anchor mix: majority brand ("emSphere", "Emsphere Technologies", domain), then URL, then generic, with descriptive keyword anchors under about 10-15% (unverified guideline, not measured for this site).
- Point links at money pages (/human-resource-management-system, /contract-labour-management-system-software, /empay-management-solution), not just the homepage.
- Targets (judgement, not data): 25-40 new unique referring domains in 6 months; show a measurable move in CC PageRank rank in the next quarterly release.
