COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Shift and Overtime Management module.

How does emSphere stop contractors from claiming unworked overtime?

Overtime is only computed if two conditions match: the worker must have physical biometric gate logs verifying the extra stay, AND the shift supervisor must have pre-approved the OT requisition.

Can the system automatically calculate double OT rates for holiday work?

Yes. The rules engine allows you to configure standard single OT rates, double OT for national/festival holidays, and special rest-day compensatory off logic strictly adhering to the Factories Act.

What happens if a worker punches in for a shift they were not scheduled for?

emSphere’s intelligent shift engine can either auto-assign the active shift window based on predefined rules or flag the attendance punch for supervisor verification before recording attendance.

How does this module help reduce monthly contractor billing costs?

By eliminating rounded-off minutes, unauthorized post-shift presence, and duplicate overtime claims, clients typically see an immediate 10% to 15% reduction in total contract labour expenditure.
