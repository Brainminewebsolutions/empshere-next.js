COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Manpower Planning & Analytics.

How does the system prevent contractors from supplying excess workers?

When a manpower indent is raised and approved, that exact quota is passed to the gate access control system. Once the approved number of workers punch in for that contractor and shift, subsequent workers are locked out at the gate.

Can we track manpower deployment by specific shop floor production lines?

Yes. emSphere allows you to assign workers directly to bays, machines, and cost centers, giving operations managers real-time visibility into line-wise staffing and productivity.

How does predictive absenteeism forecasting work?

emSphere analyzes historical attendance trends across seasons, holidays, and regional festivals, providing predictive buffer recommendations to ensure your production lines never face unexpected shortages.

Can this module integrate with our ERP's production schedule?

Yes. emSphere integrates with SAP, Oracle, and leading MES systems to ingest daily production targets and automatically calculate standard manpower requirements.
