COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Wages & Contractor Billing.

How does emSphere resolve discrepancies between contractor invoices and biometric attendance?

emSphere automatically compares the contractor’s submitted hours against the physical biometric punch logs at the plant gates. Any discrepancies (such as unworked hours, phantom shifts, or unapproved overtime) are highlighted with automated debit note calculations.

Can the system handle dynamic state minimum wage revisions?

Yes. emSphere maintains an updated state-wise minimum wage engine with configurable basic and VDA components across all skill tiers, automatically applying updated wage rates upon notification.

How does the system ensure statutory compliance before payment release?

The billing engine enforces a compliance checkpoint: monthly invoice clearance is conditionally held until the contractor uploads verified PF ECR challans, ESIC contribution proofs, and worker bank payout statements for the preceding billing cycle.

Can we export certified statutory wage registers for labor inspections?

Yes. You can instantly export certified Form 17 (Register of Wages), Form 18 (Deductions), Form 19 (Wage Slips), and CLRA Form 25 with full audit history.
