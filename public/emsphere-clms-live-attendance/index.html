COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Live Attendance & Compliance module.

How does emSphere prevent co-employment risks during attendance logging?

emSphere strictly isolates contract labour records from permanent employees. The system handles contract workers under vendor work orders and statutory registers rather than standard company payroll tables.

What happens at the gate if a contractor's statutory compliance expires?

The system issues advance alerts to the contractor and HR. If compliance (e.g., labor license, PF/ESIC proof) is not submitted before the expiration date, gate access is automatically locked for that vendor's workers.

Can this module integrate with our existing turnstiles and biometric hardware?

Yes. emSphere is hardware-agnostic and connects with standard biometric terminals, RFID card systems, face readers, and turnstiles already installed at your plant gates.

How does the system help during a Labour Inspector visit?

You can instantly export certified, inspection-ready digital registers including Form 25 (Muster Roll), Form 13 (Register of Workmen), and wage deduction summaries with timestamped logs.
