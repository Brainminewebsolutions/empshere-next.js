COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Contractor & Employee Master module.

How does the system prevent a worker from registering under multiple contractors?

emSphere uses biometric deduplication (fingerprint / face recognition / Aadhaar token). If a contractor attempts to onboard an already registered worker, the system immediately flags the duplicate profile and blocks duplicate pass creation.

Can contractors directly upload documents into the system?

Yes. emSphere provides a secure Contractor Portal where agencies can submit candidate KYC, medical fitness certificates, and license renewals for plant HR approval.

How does the master database support statutory labor audits?

All contractor licenses, worker deployment logs, and KYC data are permanently stored in an audit-ready format. You can export statutory Form 12 and Form 13 with single-click ease.

What happens when a contractor exceeds the work order headcount limit?

The system enforces hard ceiling controls. Once the allotted worker quota under a work order is reached, additional worker registrations are automatically blocked until an amendment is approved.
